# Apple Developer enrollment for Neuralytics Tech

Enroll **Neuralytics Tech (Private) Limited as an organization**, using its exact registered legal name. This is the appropriate direction for keeping your wallet and future company-owned applications under one company membership. A GitHub account, domain name, or SECP incorporation alone does not create an Apple membership or establish App Store approval.

This is planning guidance. Apple's pages could not be fetched from the build environment (HTTP 403), so verify the current requirements, availability, fees, and review rules at the official links below before applying.

## Before enrollment

1. **Verify the legal identity.** Have your SECP incorporation documents, current registered address, legal name, company telephone number, and authorized representative details available. Apple may request supporting documentation. Match the spelling and address across records; do not substitute a brand or product name for the legal entity.
2. **Find or obtain the company's D‑U‑N‑S Number.** Apple's organization enrollment generally requires this nine-digit business identifier for nongovernment organizations. An SECP incorporation/registration number is a different identifier. Use Apple's lookup/request route and resolve discrepancies in Dun & Bradstreet's record before enrolling. Do not create a duplicate listing merely because the first search is unsuccessful; new or amended records can take time to reach Apple.
3. **Publish the company website.** Make `https://neuralyticstech.com` work publicly over HTTPS with the company's identity, actual work, and a working contact address. This site is designed to provide those details; Apple makes its own verification decision.
4. **Activate a company-domain work email.** For example, `contact@neuralyticstech.com` or a named work mailbox. The current Gmail contact works for a preliminary site, but plan to use a work email associated with the company domain for organization enrollment.
5. **Choose the authorized Account Holder.** This should be a person with authority to bind the company to Apple's agreements. Use that person's Apple Account with two-factor authentication, accurate identity details, and a reachable telephone number. If enrolling as an employee, prepare an authorized reference who can confirm that authority.

## Apply and organize the account

Start at [Apple Developer Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and select the organization/company option. Supply the exact legal entity and business identifier, complete any verification Apple requests, accept the agreements, and pay the applicable membership fee. Apple's standard program fee has been US$99 per membership year, with local currency, tax, and availability subject to Apple's current terms.

Under organization enrollment, the legal entity is used for the seller identity. Developer display-name choices have separate rules; verify them before creating your first app. Each future app can have its own product name, bundle identifier, App Store listing, support URL, and privacy policy within the company's membership.

Use App Store Connect roles to invite developers and other collaborators through their own accounts. Keep certificates, signing access, and account recovery under company control. If you already have an individual membership, ask Apple about conversion or app transfer before creating overlapping accounts; eligibility and transfer restrictions apply.

## Additional preparation for the Bittensor wallet

Organization enrollment is separate from App Store review. Describe the wallet's actual features accurately in the application, review notes, and marketing.

- **Cryptocurrency rules:** Read section **3.1.5, Cryptocurrencies**, of the current App Review Guidelines. Apple has required cryptocurrency wallet apps to be offered by organization-enrolled developers. Trading/exchange functions can trigger additional conditions concerning approved exchanges and appropriate licensing/permissions in the countries where the app is offered. Self-custody does not, by itself, remove those conditions.
- **Scope and markets:** Determine whether the actual implementation enables transfers, subnet-token swaps, staking, exchange services, or other financial functions, and obtain appropriate legal advice on Pakistan and each intended distribution market. Define the supported countries based on that analysis. Do not assume that SECP company incorporation is a financial-services authorization.
- **Privacy and support:** Publish an app-specific privacy policy describing the real behavior of the app, including any analytics, crash reporting, RPC providers, wallet-address handling, and third-party services. Complete App Store privacy disclosures accurately. Add a working product support page and contact address before submission. This repository's privacy page is only for the company website.
- **User experience and review:** Explain self-custody, key recovery limitations, transaction fees, and relevant trading risks clearly in the product. Provide Apple the review instructions and access needed to exercise the features safely. Never use real customer seed phrases or private keys for review or support.
- **Claims and assets:** Do not claim Apple or Bittensor endorsement, a completed security audit, guaranteed returns, or App Store availability without a basis. The company website describes development capabilities without advertising a particular application's availability. Future app listings and review submissions should accurately describe the product's actual features.

For other AI/ML applications, the same company membership can support distinct products. Review each application's own data practices, rights to models/data, and applicable App Store rules before launch.
