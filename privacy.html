NEURALYTICS TECH / WEBSITE PRIVACY

A clear view of your privacy.

This notice applies to the company website of Neuralytics Tech (Private) Limited. It describes this website and enquiries sent to our company email address.

Browsing this website

This is a static informational website. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or cookies to this site. Fonts, illustrations, and scripts are served with the website; there are no embedded third-party widgets.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to operate and secure its service. Please see the GitHub General Privacy Statement for its practices.

Contacting us

Email links open your email application. If you email us, we receive the address, name, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store them.

We do not sell enquiry information. We keep correspondence as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you have provided, subject to those obligations.

Scope of this notice

This notice is for the company website only. Any released application will have a separate privacy policy covering its actual features and data practices.

Questions and updates

For privacy enquiries, email neuralyticstech@gmail.com. We may update this notice when the website or our practices change.
